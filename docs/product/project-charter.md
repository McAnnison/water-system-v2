# Project Charter

**Status:** Draft for stakeholder validation  
**Product:** SDK Alkaline Water Management System  
**Version:** 0.1

## Background
The business needs a dependable way to coordinate and monitor factory production, stock, dispatch, field distribution, employees, and management. Current processes, pain points, tools, and authoritative records must be documented during discovery.

## Purpose
Deliver a secure, maintainable system that improves operational visibility, data consistency, accountability, and decision-making.

## Objectives
- Capture production and stock activity accurately.
- Trace stock movement and dispatch.
- Give each role only the access it requires.
- Provide reliable reports and audit history.
- Reduce avoidable manual reconciliation.

Success measures and baselines must be agreed with stakeholders.

## Initial scope candidates
- Identity, employee accounts, roles, and permissions.
- Production records and summaries.
- Inventory, stock movements, adjustments, and reconciliation.
- Dispatch and distribution.
- Finance-related operational records.
- Notifications, reporting, and audit history.

These are candidates, not approved requirements. Discovery determines first-release scope.

## Out of scope pending approval
Payroll, statutory accounting/tax filing, ecommerce, payment processing, GPS fleet tracking, native mobile apps, and external integrations.

## Stakeholders to validate
Executive sponsor, administrator, factory supervisor, field manager, operational staff, finance/accounts personnel, and any additional process owners identified.

## Constraints and assumptions
Confirm devices, connectivity, hosting, data migration, retention, support, and recovery expectations. Business rules require process-owner approval.

## Delivery approach
Discovery → requirements baseline → design → engineering foundation → incremental implementation → verification/UAT → deployment and handover.

## Key risks
Unvalidated business rules, inaccurate source records, shared devices, weak permissions, scope growth, limited stakeholder availability, and insufficient training or recovery planning.
