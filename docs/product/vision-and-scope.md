# Product Vision and Scope

## Vision
Give SDK Alkaline Water Limited a dependable operational view of what is produced, what is in stock, what moves out, who is responsible, and where management attention is required.

## Intended users (provisional)
Executive leadership, system administrators, factory supervisors, field/distribution managers, operational staff, and finance/accounts staff. Confirm actual job functions and responsibilities during discovery.

## Product principles
1. Operational accuracy: records reflect business events and corrections remain traceable.
2. Accountability: important actions have an identifiable actor and timestamp.
3. Least privilege: users access only the information and actions needed for their duties.
4. Usability: common tasks work on real business devices and constrained connectivity.
5. Resilience: backup, recovery, and failure handling are product requirements.
6. Evidence-led design: workflows are validated with the people who perform them.

## Candidate capabilities
Identity and access; production; inventory; dispatch and distribution; finance-related operations; notifications; reporting and audit.

## Scope decisions still required
Product types and units; production inputs/outputs/wastage; stock locations and transfers; dispatch, returns and damaged goods; financial events and approval limits; employee lifecycle; report definitions; offline behavior; integrations; and data migration.

## Release strategy
Prioritize a narrow, end-to-end workflow that can be tested with real users. Set MVP boundaries after workflow mapping and impact/effort prioritization.
